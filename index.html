REVERSE ENGINEERING · PERSONAL PROJECT

Brady Roll Refill Box

I got frustrated with a Brady label printer that would stop using a roll even though there were still labels left.

I investigated how the printer tracked its media, reverse-engineered the roll’s DS2431 EEPROM-based Smart Cell and built an Arduino-based tool that reads the roll, validates its data and restores the remaining label count or roll length.

The final tool automatically detects the media, checks that it can safely be modified and verifies the EEPROM after writing.

Arduino EEPROM DS2431 Reverse engineering Electronics 3D printing
04

CONTROL SYSTEMS · MSc THESIS

Autonomous Marine Docking

For my MSc thesis at CPAC Systems, I co-developed an autonomous docking system for marine vessels using Model Predictive Control.

We developed and simulated the system in Python, progressed to CPAC’s boat simulator and finally validated it on a real vessel at sea.

The work resulted in granted Swedish patent SE2450627-1, and the patented solution is still used at CPAC today.
