How I work

Break it down, build early and integrate often.

When I face a new technical problem, I usually start by breaking it into smaller subproblems. From there, I work out what each part needs to do, what information or signals it depends on and how the pieces need to fit together. I like getting practical early, so I prototype, test and integrate rather than waiting for every part to feel finished in isolation.

I am strongest in prototyping, system integration and troubleshooting physical systems. Over the next few years I want to deepen my control-theory knowledge and keep building systems that have to function outside a controlled lab environment.
